# Solar project working rule

Dave expects continuous, practical progress toward his working AlphaESS solar app. When a task is authorized, carry it through implementation and the closest meaningful verification; do not end a turn after a proposal, a partial setup, or a report when further authorized work is possible. Give brief progress updates during longer work and proceed through routine choices independently. Ask only when essential information or permission is actually required. Preserve the existing app, user files, and higher-priority safety and approval requirements.

Use the real house reference in `references/house-front-reference.png` as the primary visual source. It is the only real house image available; do not ask Dave to find more. The unseen sides, roof, and panel placement are estimates. Do not describe a photo reference or a rough model as a finished, accurate 3D model. Keep the house reference private unless explicitly authorized for publication or external transmission.

Dave rejected the procedural house studies as substantially worse than the earlier concept images. Do not produce more primitive/blockout variants or present those studies as progress towards a publishable visual. Preserve the files and pursue an appropriately realistic tool and workflow.

Project context and current limitations are in `HANDOFF.md` and the shared `alphaess-solar` worklog. The checked upstream sources, licence restrictions and integration notes are in `GITHUB-RESEARCH.md`. Keep these current when substantial work changes the state of the project.
